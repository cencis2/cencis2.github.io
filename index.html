FAQs

What type of power outlets are used on Taniti?

▼

►

Power outlets are 120 volts, which is the exact same standard as used in the United States.

What are the alcohol sale laws and legal drinking age?

▼

►

The legal drinking age on Taniti is 18 (though it is not strictly enforced). However, please note that alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold anywhere on the island between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Do people speak English on Taniti?

▼

►

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. However, very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents, so it is helpful to have a translation app handy when exploring.

Is there medical care available on the island?

▼

►

Yes. There is one main hospital and several smaller medical clinics. For peace of mind, the hospital features many multilingual employees to assist international visitors.

Is Taniti safe for tourists?

▼

►

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. However, as tourism has grown, there have been increasing reports of pickpocketing and other petty theft. We recommend keeping an eye on your belongings in crowded areas.

Do holiday closures affect tourist areas?

▼

►

Yes. Taniti enjoys a large number of national holidays. Many tourist attractions and local restaurants close on these days, so it is highly recommended to check local calendars and plan your schedule accordingly.

What currency is accepted on Taniti, and can I use credit cards?

▼

►

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar ($) as its official currency, but many local businesses also accept euros and yen. Major credit cards are widely accepted, and there are several banks available across the island to facilitate currency exchange.
